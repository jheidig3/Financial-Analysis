====================================================================
QUANTITATIVE OPTIONS STRATEGY & MODELING GUIDELINES
====================================================================

1. LEAPS BUYING STRATEGY (LONG CALLS AS STOCK REPLACEMENT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Expiration Window: 365+ Days to Expiration (DTE) / 1+ Year Minimum.
- Delta Target: >= 0.80 Delta (Deep In-The-Money).
- Core Advantage: High stock sensitivity (delta) with significantly 
  lower capital outlay compared to buying 100 shares outright.
- Management: Roll out/up when DTE drops below 180 or delta drops 
  below 0.70.

2. AUTOMATED OPTION SELLING (COVERED CALLS & CASH-SECURED PUTS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Selling Window: 30 to 45 Days to Expiration (DTE).
- Delta Target: 0.15 to 0.30 Delta (Out-Of-The-Money).
- PoP Target: Probability of Profit >= 70%.
- Annualized Premium Yield Target: >= 15% on risk capital.
- Management: Close or roll at 50% max profit or 21 DTE.

3. QUANTITATIVE SELLER RATING SCALE (1 TO 6)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Score 6 (Optimal): IV Percentile > 50%, DTE 30-45, Delta 0.15-0.25, 
  no earnings/binary risk before expiration. High risk-adjusted return.
- Score 4-5 (Favorable): Moderate IV, acceptable delta, solid yield.
- Score 1-3 (Avoid): Low IV Percentile (<20%), low return on capital, 
  imminent earnings announcement or high event risk.

4. CONGRESSIONAL TRADE & FLOW TRACKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Monitor insider/congressional disclosures for unusual volume and 
  directional bias.
- Cross-reference politician transaction dates against institutional 
  option volume and IV spikes.
