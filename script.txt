====================================================================
QUANTITATIVE OPTIONS STRATEGY & MODELING GUIDELINES
====================================================================

1. LEAPS BUYING STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Primary Target: Long-term calls acting as stock replacement.
- Expiration Window: 365+ Days to Expiration (DTE) / 1+ Year Minimum.
- Target Delta: >= 0.80 Delta (Deep In-The-Money).
- Core Advantage: High stock sensitivity with significantly lower 
  capital outlay compared to buying 100 shares of underlying equity.

2. AUTOMATED OPTION SELLING (CSP / COVERED CALLS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Selling Window: 30 to 45 Days to Expiration (DTE).
- Delta Target: 0.15 to 0.30 Delta (Out-Of-The-Money).
- Key Metrics: High Probability of Profit (PoP >= 70%), 
  Annualized Premium Yield >= 15%.

3. QUANTITATIVE SELLER RATING SCALE (1 TO 6)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Score 6 (Optimal): High IV Percentile (>50%), DTE 30-45, 
  Delta 0.15-0.25, no earnings/binary risk within expiration.
- Score 4-5 (Favorable): Moderate IV, acceptable delta, solid yield.
- Score 1-3 (Avoid): Low IV Percentile, low return on capital, 
  imminent earnings announcement or event risk.
